About Giftess

Giftess was born out of a simple belief — that every gift should tell a story. We are a passionate team of gifting experts based in India, dedicated to crafting premium, personalised gift hampers that leave a lasting impression.

From birthdays and anniversaries to Valentine's Day and custom corporate hampers, we curate every hamper with love, quality products, and beautiful packaging. Our mission is to make gifting effortless, memorable, and truly special for every occasion.

We source only the finest products — chocolates, skincare, candles, dry fruits, and more — to ensure every recipient feels valued and celebrated.

Refund & Return Policy

Our Policy

At Giftess, we are committed to ensuring your complete satisfaction. We take great care in preparing and packaging every hamper.

Eligibility for Refunds

  • Refund requests must be made within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Products must be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging.
  • Personalised and custom hampers are non-refundable once confirmed.
  • Perishable items (chocolates, food products) are non-refundable.

Damaged or Wrong Items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, please contact us on WhatsApp within 24 hours with photos. We will arrange a replacement or full refund.

How to Request a Refund

Contact us on WhatsApp at +91 6002698296 with your Order ID and photos (if applicable). Our team will respond within 24 hours.

Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.
