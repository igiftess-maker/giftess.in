Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At Giftess, your satisfaction is our top priority. We take great pride in creating beautiful, carefully curated gift hampers. We understand that sometimes things don't go as expected, and we are committed to making it right.

Eligibility for Returns & Refunds

We accept return and refund requests under the following conditions:

  • Requests must be made within 48 hours of receiving your order.
  • Products must be unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging.
  • You must provide your Order ID and photos of the item(s) if reporting damage.
  • Personalised and custom hampers are non-refundable once production has begun.
  • Perishable items such as chocolates, food products, and flowers are non-refundable.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, please contact us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery with:

  • Your Order ID
  • Clear photos of the damaged/incorrect item(s)
  • A brief description of the issue

We will arrange a free replacement or a full refund at no additional cost to you.

Non-Refundable Items

  • Custom-made or personalised hampers after order confirmation
  • Perishable items (chocolates, food, flowers)
  • Items that have been used or are not in original condition
  • Orders where delivery failed due to incorrect address provided by customer

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or return, please reach us on WhatsApp at +91 6002698296 with your Order ID and relevant photos. Our customer support team will respond within 24 hours (Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 9 PM IST).

Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days. The refund will be credited back to your original payment method. Bank processing times may vary.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placing them, provided the order has not yet been dispatched. To cancel, contact us immediately on WhatsApp with your Order ID.

Contact Us

We are committed to resolving all issues fairly and promptly. Please don't hesitate to reach out.
